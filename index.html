How the numbers are calculated
COD orders = Total − PrepaidCOD delivered = Delivered − Prepaid. COD RTO = COD orders − COD delivered.
RTO charges = COD RTO × RTO costDeducted from COD margin, not from the supplier payment.
COD margin = Delivered × (price − supplier cost) − RTO chargesSupplier cost is netted out of COD orders directly.
Net prepaid settlement = Prepaid price − gateway fee − GST on feeRazorpay fee is charged per prepaid order.
Supplier payment = Prepaid orders × supplier costPaid separately, since prepaid revenue arrives gross.
Break-even CPP = (Cash in − supplier payment − other expenses) ÷ 1.18 ÷ Total ordersThe highest ex-GST Meta CPP that keeps net cash at zero.
Output GST = (COD delivered × price + Prepaid orders × price) × 18⁄118GST on revenue, extracted from GST-inclusive prices.
Input GST (ITC) = 18% of ad spend + gateway GST × prepaid orders + 18⁄118 of (supplier cost × delivered) + 18⁄118 of other expensesClaimable input credit.
All figures assume 18% GST throughout, matching the Sheets model. This is a working calculator, not filed tax or accounting advice.
